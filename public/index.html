How to play

Pocket Garden is meant to sit quietly beside whatever else you are doing. Plant a seed, wander off, and come back later — your plants keep growing in real time, even if you close the tab.

  • Tap an empty plot to plant a seed.
  • Tap Water, then a growing plant, to shave a little time off its wait. Each plant can be watered once.
  • Harvest when a plant is ready to earn coins.
  • Spend coins in the shop to unlock new plants and extra plots.

Your garden is saved on this device. The tip jar is optional and does not change the game, aside from a small thank-you cosmetic if a payment goes through.
